# Motion

Motion is stepped, finite, skippable and fails open, and every effect has a still (L4). The only
endless motion is the rain in its three forms, and Hold rain stops all of it. The catalogue of
every motion, with its still, is in `05-a11y-motion.md`; each effect is shown beside its still on
its own page as it is built. These are the timing and layer tokens.
