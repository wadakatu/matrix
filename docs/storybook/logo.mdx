# Logo

The mark is by Chisato Satoh and is used on wadakatu.dev with permission. **It is not covered by
the MIT licence and may not be reused** (`assets/logo/NOTICE.md`); it is not in the package.

<img src="assets/logo/logo-400.png" width="200" height="200" alt="wadakatu's mark" />

- **Always the designer's file, whole** (L5): square, its black field included.
- **Sizes:** 44px in the header, 32px in the compact header, fluid in the fetch card and the boot.
- **The only treatment** is the operator-night tint, a filter that moves no pixel.
- **Forbidden:** cropping, rounding, rotating, masking, any transform, `object-fit: cover`,
  redrawing, or setting it in glyphs.
